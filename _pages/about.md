<section class="section" id="bio">
  <div class="section__head">
    <h2 class="section__title">About</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="about-text">
    <p>
      I am a <strong>Machine Learning Research Scientist</strong> at <a href="https://www.natera.com/">Natera</a> in the Therapeutics AI team, where I focus on developing <strong>genomic large language models</strong> for clinical applications and designing deep learning methods for <strong>neoantigen prediction in cancer immunotherapy</strong>.
    </p>
    <p>
      I obtained my PhD at the <a href="http://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/dascim/">Data Science and Mining Team</a> of the Computer Science Laboratory at <a href="https://www.polytechnique.edu/en">École Polytechnique</a>, under the supervision of Prof. <a href="http://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/~mvazirg/">Michalis Vazirgiannis</a>. In 2024, I completed a research internship at <a href="https://www.instadeep.com/">InstaDeep</a> working on graph neural networks and single-cell foundation models. In 2022, I interned at the <a href="https://www.simonsfoundation.org/flatiron/">Flatiron Institute</a> of the Simons Foundation in New York, applying graph neural networks to cancer-gene prediction with Dr. <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=YDl3mfgAAAAJ&hl=en">Zijun Frank Zhang</a>.
    </p>
    <p>
      Earlier, I graduated from the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the <a href="https://www.ece.ntua.gr/en">National Technical University of Athens</a>. As an undergraduate I worked at the <a href="http://erga.di.uoa.gr/">Laboratory of Algebraic and Geometric Algorithms</a> at the <a href="https://www.di.uoa.gr/en">National and Kapodistrian University of Athens</a> with Prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.gr/citations?user=ZK6y-cIAAAAJ&hl=en">Ioannis Emiris</a>, and completed Google Summer of Code 2019.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>
